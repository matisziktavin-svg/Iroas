## Core Training Principles (2025 Research-Based)

### 1. Progressive Overload — The Foundation

**Research findings:**
- Both load progression and repetition progression produce similar hypertrophy gains
- Rectus femoris growth slightly favors rep progression
- Dynamic strength gains slightly favor load progression
- No significant differences in other outcomes (muscle thickness, endurance, body comp)

**Practical application:**
```
OPTION A: Load Progression
Week 1-2: 3x10 @ 60% 1RM
Week 3-4: 3x10 @ 65% 1RM
Week 5-6: 3x10 @ 70% 1RM

OPTION B: Repetition Progression
Week 1-2: 3x8 @ 70% 1RM
Week 3-4: 3x10 @ 70% 1RM
Week 5-6: 3x12 @ 70% 1RM

Both work. Choose based on preference and training context.
```

**Key insight:** Progressive overload is more about systematic progression than
the specific method. Consistency matters more than perfection.

### 2. Hypertrophy Mechanisms — The Three Pillars

**Mechanical Tension (primary driver):** force on the muscle during contraction.
Maximize with challenging loads (60-85% 1RM), full ROM, controlled eccentric,
40-70 sec time under tension per set.

**Metabolic Stress (secondary):** metabolite buildup (lactate, H+, inorganic
phosphate). Moderate loads (60-70% 1RM), higher reps (12-20+), short rest
(30-60s), techniques like drop sets / BFR.

**Muscle Damage (tertiary):** microscopic fiber tears. Emphasize the eccentric,
occasional novel patterns, full stretch positions. *Don't chase soreness — it's
not required for growth.*

### 3. Training Frequency (2025 consensus)

```
MINIMUM: 2x per muscle group per week
OPTIMAL: 2-3x per muscle group per week
ADVANCED: 4-6x per muscle group per week (if volume managed)
```
Rationale: muscle protein synthesis peaks 24-48h post-training, then returns to
baseline — more frequency captures more growth windows.

**Sample splits:**
```
2x (Upper/Lower):  Mon Upper / Tue Lower / Thu Upper / Fri Lower
3x (Push/Pull/Legs): Mon Push / Tue Pull / Wed Legs / Thu Push / Fri Pull / Sat Legs
4-6x (Daily Undulating): spread muscle groups across the week at varying intensities
```

### 4. Volume Landmarks (per muscle group, per week)

```
MINIMUM EFFECTIVE VOLUME (MEV): 10-12 sets
Target volume for most:         12-20 sets
MAXIMUM RECOVERABLE VOLUME (MRV): 20-25+ sets (individual)
```
Start at MEV, gradually increase; monitor recovery (sleep, performance,
soreness); deload when approaching MRV (reduce volume 40-50% for 1 week).

Example chest week = Bench 4 + Incline DB 3 + Cable Fly 3 + Dips 3 = **13 sets**.

### 5. Intensity Zones

| Zone | Reps | Load | Rest | Purpose | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Strength | 1-6 | 85-100% 1RM | 3-5 min | Neural, max force | Compounds |
| Hypertrophy | 6-12 | 70-85% 1RM | 2-3 min | Growth sweet spot | All major lifts |
| Metabolic/Pump | 12-20+ | 60-70% 1RM | 1-2 min | Metabolic stress, capacity | Isolation, finishers |

*All rep ranges build muscle when taken close to failure. Choose by goal, joint
health, and recovery capacity.*

## Exercise Selection

**Compounds first — The Big 5:** Squat (quads/glutes/core), Deadlift (posterior
chain/grip/core), Bench Press (chest/tris/shoulders), Overhead Press
(shoulders/tris/core), Row (back/biceps/rear delts). Most mass recruited, highest
tension, functional transfer, time-efficient. Program: compounds FIRST when fresh,
3-5 per session, 4-6 sets each, majority of volume from compounds.

**Isolation for refinement:** target specific muscles, address weaknesses, add
volume without systemic fatigue. After compounds, 2-4 exercises, 2-4 sets, higher
reps (10-20), shorter rest (60-90s). E.g. leg extension, leg curl, lateral raise,
curls, tricep extensions.

## Biomechanics & Form

- **Joint stacking** — load travels efficiently through aligned joints (squat: bar
  over mid-foot, knees over toes, hip/knee/ankle aligned).
- **Bracing** — deep belly breath, brace abs "like taking a punch," neutral spine,
  then execute. Protects spine, transfers force.
- **Range of motion** — full ROM unless contraindicated (pain, mobility limits, or
  a specific partial-rep strength goal). Full ROM → more lengthening, joint health.
- **Tempo** — standard 2-1-1-0 (2s eccentric, 1s pause, 1s concentric, 0 top). For
  hypertrophy emphasize the eccentric (3-4s); for strength, explosive concentric.

**Cues.** Squat: spread the floor, chest up, knees out, drive through heels.
Deadlift: lats tight/pull the slack, push the floor away, hips+shoulders rise
together, lock out with glutes. Bench: retract scapula, leg drive, bar to nipple
line, press back and up. OHP: squeeze glutes, elbows slightly forward, press up and
back, lock out with a shrug.

## Periodization

**Linear (beginners):** Wk1-4 hypertrophy (3-4x10-12) → Wk5-8 strength (4-5x5-6)
→ Wk9 deload (−50% volume) → repeat, progress loads.

**Daily Undulating (int/adv):** Mon heavy (3x5 @85%) / Wed light (3x12 @65%) / Fri
moderate (3x8 @75%). Frequent variation, multiple stimuli.

**Block (advanced):** Accumulation (3-4wk, high volume) → Intensification (2-3wk,
high intensity) → Realization (1-2wk, test/peak, very low volume).

## Recovery & Adaptation

- **Sleep (non-negotiable):** min 7h, optimal 8-9h for hard trainers. Drives MPS,
  hormones, CNS recovery, glycogen. Poor sleep = poor gains, period.
- **Nutrition timing:** pre (1-2h): 20-40g protein + 40-80g carbs; post (within 2h):
  25-40g protein + 50-100g carbs. Total daily intake matters most; timing optimizes.
- **Deload:** every 4-8 weeks or on plateau / excess soreness / disrupted sleep /
  low motivation. Reduce volume 40-50%, maintain intensity, 3-7 days.
- **Active recovery:** light cardio, mobility, post-training stretching, foam
  rolling, swimming/yoga — blood flow without damage.

## Programming Examples

**Beginner Full Body (3x/wk, alternate A/B Mon/Wed/Fri):**
```
A: Squat 3x8 · Bench 3x8 · Barbell Row 3x8 · OHP 2x10 · Leg Curl 2x12 · Curl 2x12
B: Deadlift 3x6 · Incline DB 3x10 · Pull-ups 3xAMRAP · Lateral Raise 3x12 · Leg Ext 2x15 · Tri Ext 2x12
```

**Intermediate Push/Pull/Legs (6x/wk):**
```
PUSH: Bench 4x8 · OHP 3x10 · Incline DB 3x10 · Lateral Raise 3x12 · Dips 3x10 · Cable Fly 2x15
PULL: Deadlift 4x6 · Pull-ups 4x8 · Row 3x10 · Face Pulls 3x15 · Curl 3x12 · Rear Delt Fly 2x15
LEGS: Squat 4x8 · RDL 3x10 · Leg Press 3x12 · Leg Curl 3x12 · Leg Ext 3x15 · Calf Raise 4x15
```

**Advanced Upper/Lower (4x/wk):**
```
UPPER POWER: Bench 5x5 · Row 5x5 · OHP 4x6 · Weighted Pull-up 4x6
LOWER POWER: Squat 5x5 · Deadlift 5x5 · Front Squat 3x6
UPPER HYPERTROPHY: Incline Press 4x10 · Cable Row 4x12 · DB Press 3x12 · Lat Pulldown 3x12 · Lateral Raise 3x15 · Bi/Tri 6 sets each
LOWER HYPERTROPHY: RDL 4x10 · Bulgarian Split Squat 3x12 · Leg Press 3x15 · Leg Curl 3x15 · Leg Ext 3x15
```

## Common Mistakes

1. **Chasing soreness** — DOMS ≠ growth. Judge by performance + measurements.
2. **Ego lifting** — controllable weight through full ROM beats heavy & sloppy.
3. **Neglecting progression** — track workouts; add weight/reps/sets every 1-2 wks.
4. **Random programming** — run a program 8-12 weeks before switching.
5. **Skipping compounds** — build around squat/deadlift/press/row.

## Advanced Techniques (use sparingly)

- **Drop sets** — final isolation set: to failure → −20-30% → failure → repeat.
- **Rest-pause** — set to failure → rest 15-20s → 2-3 more reps → repeat 2-3x.
- **BFR** — light cuffs at 50-70% occlusion, 30% 1RM for 15-30 reps; similar
  hypertrophy at low load; good for injury recovery.
- **Cluster sets** — heavy, low reps with short intra-set rest (e.g. 5 reps with
  10s rest between each).

## Injury Prevention

- **Warm-up:** general 5-10 min (light cardio + dynamic stretch) then specific 5-10
  min (activation + ramping sets: empty bar → 50% → 70% → 90% → work sets).
- **Load management rule:** don't increase volume AND intensity simultaneously.
  Safe: +reps at same weight, or +weight at fewer reps, or +sets at lower intensity.
- **Good vs bad pain:** good = muscle burn/pump/next-day soreness. Bad = sharp,
  joint, one-sided, or increasing during the set → stop, assess, modify or skip.

## Testing & Assessment

- **Strength (every 8-12 wks):** true 1RM (experienced only), or 3RM/5RM, or AMRAP
  at submaximal weight → track progress, set percentages, find weaknesses.
- **Body composition (every 4 wks):** DEXA > Bod Pod > scale + measurements +
  photos. Track weight, BF%, muscle measurements, progress photos.
- **Performance markers:** rep PRs, volume PRs, density (same volume less time),
  work capacity (recovery between sets).

## Final Principles

1. **Consistency > perfection** — the imperfect program done consistently wins.
2. **Progressive overload is king** — not stronger / more volume over time = not growing.
3. **Recovery is training** — muscles grow during rest; prioritize sleep + nutrition.
4. **Form before load** — clean reps build more and prevent injury.
5. **Train for longevity** — the best program is one sustainable for years without injury.
